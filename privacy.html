Privacy notice

Gill Family Media · last updated 7 October 2026

Gill Family Media is a private app used by one household on its own Android TV and phone. It isn't sold, advertised or offered to anyone else.

Google account information

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app uses:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, only to show your profile inside the app.
  • A hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission), only to keep the app's own data in step between your own devices: watch progress, saved titles and app settings. The app can't see or change any of your other Drive files.

What happens to it

  • Google account information is used only for the purposes above.
  • It isn't sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Sign-in tokens and passwords are stored encrypted on your own devices.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account under Third-party apps and services. To delete the app's hidden data too, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, and choose "Delete hidden app data" for Gill Family Media.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
